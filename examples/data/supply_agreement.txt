SUPPLY AGREEMENT (SUMMARY)

1. Scope
This agreement governs the supply of goods between the Seller and the Buyer.

2. Delivery
The Seller shall deliver the goods no later than 15 March 2026.
The Seller shall provide the delivery note with each shipment.
The Buyer may reject goods that do not conform to the specification.

3. Payment
The Buyer shall pay the invoice within 30 days of receipt.
The Buyer shall not withhold payment without a written notice.
Late payment shall incur a penalty of USD 5,000.
The Seller may charge interest on overdue amounts.

4. Confidentiality
The Seller shall not disclose confidential information to third parties.
The Buyer must keep the pricing terms confidential.
Disclosure of confidential information is prohibited.

5. Documents
The Seller must submit the certificate of origin with the invoice.
The Buyer is required to acknowledge receipt in writing.

6. Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement with 60 days written notice.
The agreement is defined as the entire understanding of the parties.

7. Governing Law
This agreement shall be governed by the laws of Turkey.
